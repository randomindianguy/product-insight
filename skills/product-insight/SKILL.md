# Product Insight

A hiring manager is pricing the risk of your future decisions. Their only evidence is your past ones. An interview samples them under a clock, and the sample can be rehearsed. A **receipt** cannot: it either ran or it didn't, and they can check.

Three sessions, in order, each in a fresh context window.

1. `/thesis` — find a claim about the company that survives being killed.
2. `/artifact` — build the receipt that tests the claim.
3. `/ship-review` — get it read.

The write-up belongs to `/ship-review`, after the build has produced a result. Reach it only through `/artifact`.

`/claims` runs standalone on any sentence about your own work: a resume bullet, a cold email, a post.
