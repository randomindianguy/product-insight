# PROFILE.template.md

Copy this to `PROFILE.md` (which is gitignored) and fill it in. `/thesis` Step 6
reads your `PROFILE.md` to audit the models *you* import from your prior domain,
instead of guessing.

Keep it narrow. It holds only what the transfer test needs: the domains you came
from, the conditions that made your instincts true there, and the models you reach
for. Not a résumé. If it reads like a bio, the skill will skim it. A tight list of
"here are my imports and the conditions under them" is what it can actually use.

Delete every instruction line once you've filled your own in.

---

## Prior domains — with the conditions underneath

The transfer test doesn't care *that* you worked in a domain. It cares *what was
true there that may not be true where you're aiming.* Name the conditions — they are
what transfer or break.

**[Domain, years, companies]**
Conditions that made your instincts true there:
- [e.g. high-consideration purchase — buyer deliberates, tolerates friction]
- [e.g. long sales cycle — trust builds over months, a page has time]
- [e.g. the user and the buyer are the same person]
- [name the conditions specific to your domain, not its achievements]

→ When you reach for one of these instincts at a company in a different domain,
Step 6 checks whether the condition underneath it still holds. It often won't. That
break is the thing the audit exists to catch.
